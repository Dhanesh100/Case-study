# V11 review — it is not a word problem, it is a weighting problem

**12 October 2026** · Review of V11 of the CoverSure Policy Portfolio case study
· 35 agents across two passes, 30 of them challenging the findings of the other five

**Read it as a page:** <https://dhanesh100.github.io/Case-study/review-v11/>
**The version under review:** <https://dhanesh100.github.io/Case-study/#v11>

---

## 00 · Correction first

V11 was published with the claim that its surface was **2,120 words against V10's 2,347** — a
227-word reduction. **That was wrong.** The measuring script stripped `<svg>` before counting,
hiding ~470 words of diagram labels, and the engine simulator's copy lives in the script block
where it was never seen at all.

Counted like for like, **V11 is roughly 40 words heavier than V10, not 227 lighter.** There was
no reduction.

`build-copy.py` had the same blind spots plus a third: no handler for `dl.v10-dec`, so **all five
decision blocks were missing from `COPY-V11.md`** — a file whose own header claims it cannot
drift from what is published. It had drifted by 676 words. Both are fixed.

> The lesson for any future measurement here: the surface is not just `<p>` tags. It is SVG text,
> table cells, definition lists, and copy that lives in JavaScript. Count all of it, or do not
> quote a number.

## 01 · The diagnosis

| | |
|---|---|
| Bold runs | **64** across 11 sections — §05 alone has 14 |
| Scan layer | **590 words** of heading, bold and label |
| Time to skim it | **2 min 34 s** at 230 wpm |
| What a founder actually gives | **60 seconds** — about 230 words |

The layer that exists to be skimmed is **2.6× over the budget of the person it was built for.**
Headings are not the problem — all eleven total only 94 words. Bold text totals 468. §01 carries
a single 26-word sentence entirely in bold.

The second finding matters more. **The surface carries paraphrase; the See-more panels carry the
judgement.** The sharpest line in the case study — *"not where to place a CTA"* — sits inside a
dialog that a scanning founder never opens.

**So the answer is not subtraction.** Every large surface cut proposed was tested and rejected:
the rule-set rows, the shipped card copy and the simulator's reasoning are each the only instance
of their content. What there is instead is ~250 words of restatement, and ~200 words of the best
thinking one click out of reach. **Substitute at roughly constant length**, then cut hard in
See-more where it costs nothing.

## 02 · The change that does the most

**One bold claim per section.** Sixty-four runs become eleven. No content leaves the page; it
stops competing for the eye.

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Bold runs | 64 | **11** |
| Scan layer | 590 words | **266 words** |
| Skim time | 2:34 | **1:09** — −55% |

## 03 · The spine — eleven headings that argue instead of describe

| § | Recommended heading | The one bold line |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **The dead click was the bug. Portfolio was the opportunity.** | The route existed — it just looked exactly like the control beside it. |
| 02 | **CoverRisk knew the cover gap. Portfolio never used it.** | Nobody browses insurance. They arrive with a reason, and land on Portfolio. |
| 03 | **Ten rules decide what Portfolio shows — and when it shows nothing.** | No rule fires on one fact. The highest-priority true condition wins. |
| 04 | **Ask for what is missing before selling what is next.** | Three things we already knew decide every recommendation. Nothing new was collected. |
| 05 | **Personalisation needs a frame that refuses to move.** | Come back in six months and it is still the screen you learned. |
| 06 | **Health only is still step one. Term life next.** | I ranked products by what they protect, not by what they earn. |
| 07 | **Without the household, every later recommendation is a guess.** | With nobody on file, the engine asks who you cover before pitching. |
| 08 | **Owning a policy should be the route into its rating.** | A number invites price comparison; a verdict plus counts can be checked. |
| 09 | **An offer never outranks the reason you opened the screen.** | Entry points are already arriving. Keeping them out is the work now. |
| 10 | **No numbers yet. Here is what would disprove the claims.** | Clicks will not prove this works. Banner stacks win clicks too. |
| 11 | **I built the rules. Product owns where each policy ranks.** | I did not build CoverRisk or KYP — I made them reach a decision. |

§10 was the only heading in V11 that described rather than argued.

---

## 04 · Show, don't tell — four places copy becomes a component

1. **§05 — the ten-row table becomes a live deck.** Reuse the simulator's phone frame: three
   slots hold still while the engine band cycles the seven shipped cards. The argument *is* the
   motion. The card copy stays — as UI inside a phone it reads as shipped evidence. **−143 words.**
2. **§03 — five toggle chips for the five signals.** The reader flips signals; matching rules
   light up and the winner rises. Turn everything off and the screen says *nothing relevant →
   nothing promoted*. They produce the constraint with their own hands. **−48 words.**
3. **§10 — an empty dashboard.** Seven metric tiles with dashes, a chip reading *awaiting first
   cohort*, and one tile struck through: **recommendation click-through — "not the goal"**. The
   honesty stops being an apology and becomes an instrument. Nothing counts up.
4. **§11 — colour the pipeline by voice.** Solid accent = *I built*, outlined = *I connected*,
   grey = *Product owns*. Three comma-chains of 143 words become a picture that answers "what did
   you own" by showing where in the system it sits. **−45 words.**

## 05 · Accuracy, not style — three claims that must change

| § | Claim | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | "the one screen almost everyone visits" | A frequency claim with no data. Replace with the structural version. |
| 05 | "Users stop scanning it and start ignoring it" | Asserts measured user behaviour. Reword to *which is how a recommendation surface turns into banner space*. |
| 10 | "More exploration… is the intended effect, not a measured one" | **Delete, do not merely unbold.** The hedge does not retract the outcome it plants, and `CONTEXT.md` §6 already names this shape among claims that must not come back. |

§02 also says *"Portfolio is where they land"* and *"Portfolio is where those visits land"* — the
same sentence twice, in the same section.

---

## 06 · Defended — what reviewers wanted to cut, and why it stays

- **§05's shipped card copy** — the only shipped-product evidence on the page, and the only gloss
  for HospiCash and Super Top-Up. It moves into the phone frame; it does not leave.
- **§03's eight rule rows** — the only evidence for "every pitch card leads with a price", and the
  only place the non-pitch *Add your policy* card appears, which is what proves the strategy is
  not selling-first.
- **The simulator's reasoning lines** — diagnosed correctly as repetitive, but deleting them
  removes the best copy on the page.
- **§07's "rather than in the pitch band"** — the only named rejected placement in the document.

Thirty verification agents challenged every proposed cut and the majority were rejected on the
grounds that they would cost design evidence or product context. **That rejection list is as much
the output of this review as the recommendations are.**

## 07 · The arithmetic

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Scan layer | 590 words · 2:34 | **266 words · 1:09** |
| Bold runs | 64 | **11** |
| Surface | 2,960 words | **~2,444** |
| See-more | 3,056 words | **~3,215** |

Depth grows, the surface shrinks, nothing is deleted. Surface length barely moves. **Evidence
density moves a lot.**

## 08 · Order of work

1. **The bold pass and the eleven headings.** Mechanical, one sitting, and it buys the entire 55%
   scan-layer reduction on its own.
2. **The three accuracy fixes.** Small, and they close the only places a sharp founder could push back.
3. **§10's empty dashboard.** The only section with no visual.
4. **§05's deck and §03's signal chips.** The two biggest builds, best done one at a time.
