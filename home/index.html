What I do

How I work

Three things I keep coming back to.

  • Use what the product already knows. The best recommendation layer collects nothing new. Most products already hold enough to be useful and simply never connect it — which makes it a design problem, not a data project.
  • Personalisation needs a frame that refuses to move. If everything can change, the screen is different on every visit and people stop reading it. Fix the structure, vary only what sits inside it.
  • Say what you cannot prove yet. Shipped is not the same as validated. I would rather publish the hypothesis and the test than imply a result that no data supports.
