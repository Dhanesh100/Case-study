The Context

The Shapoorji Pallonji Real Estate firm carries an impeccable reputation in the Indian real estate market, with a reputable history of over 150 years. Their legacy comprises of notable structures built across the country. We built a user experience for their website that was worthy of their rich legacy, that not only aimed to easily find the right contacts, but also lower the overall bounce rates.

Uncover My Role

  • Led the design of a Website from concept to delivery.
  • Worked with SPRE product team to align on needs, gather feedback, and secure approvals.
  • Managed one UI designers and coordinated with a development team of 2 engineers to ensure smooth execution.

See How We Transformed

5x

reduced cost per lead via website

680%

average increase in monthly organic leads on the new SPRE website.

Highlighting the legacy

Don’t be surprised if some of it looks familiar and inspires nostalgia. An immersive About page was designed to bring the rich legacy of Shapoorji Pallonji to the fore. This helped potential customers acquaint themselves with every unique and historic project that firm had erected over the years.

See How We Transformed

To transform the flow of the homepage, we decided to create a horizontal storyboard-like experience that highlighted projects and boosted engagement. This helped ease the navigational experience that cut down bounce rates by 60%.

An in-depth view

We designed dedicated pages for each of the projects, so prospects could learn about the unique conceptualization and thought that the projects were based on. While helping potential customers browse, it also helped them shortlist projects and submit specific inquiries to be serviced.
