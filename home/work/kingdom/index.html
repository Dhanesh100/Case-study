Building Family Bonds Through Kingdom OTT Platform for U.S. Viewers

Unlock the Kingdom OTT Insights

Innovation

Live stream merchandising with Apple Vision Pro

  • In-App Purchases: Let users buy character or player merchandise during streaming.
  • NBA Example: Enable one-tap jersey purchases during live NBA games.
  • Apple Vision Pro: Use spatial ads and virtual try-on for seamless purchases in events like UEFA Euro 2024.

Gamification

  • Gamification for Engagement: Transform viewing into an interactive experience with points, badges, leaderboards, and challenges.
  • Example: Jio’s ‘Jeeto Dhan Dhana Dhan’ lets fans win prizes per match. MeWatch could add a "Weekend Binge Challenge" (e.g., watch 5 episodes over the weekend to earn 50 bonus points for in-app shopping).
  • Deloitte Survey: 45% of U.S. gamers want more of their favorite shows and movies to include game-like experiences.

AI Assistant

  • AI Assistant to help user discover the content desired at ease.​
  • Works or assists as a friend (Virtual avatar) who can help pick the content as per user’s need with enhanced filters.​
  • Reduces the time spent in content discoverability.​

Interactive Narrative​

  • Interactive Stories with AR: Enable users to shape narrative paths in real-time using AR, enhancing engagement.
  • Example: Inspired by Netflix’s Bandersnatch, users can make plot-altering choices in MeWatch content.
  • Apple Vision Pro: Utilize gesture recognition so users can control character fates interactively, deepening story involvement.

Community-
building

Virtual Watch party​

  • Watch party allows users to watch video and chat in real-time simultaneously with friends, family, regardless of their physical location. ​
  • Example: Amazon Prime Video offers a built-in Watch Party feature for subscribers, allowing up to 100 participants to join.​
  • Mewatch  can facilitate virtual watch parties by using Apple Vison Pro where users can gather in a shared virtual space and create their own personal theatre to watch and discuss any movies/shows together. ​

Connect with Friends

  • Connect with Friends typically includes features such as sharing content to social networks, following or interacting with friends, and seeing what friends are watching.​
  • By incorporating  social media elements, Mewatch can leverage the power of social networks to attract and retain users.​
  • Example: FLYX is the first streaming social network, designed to recommend shows depending upon the trending list, friend’s suggestions and user watch history. ​

Collaborative watchlist​

  • Users can create collaborative watchlist which allows everyone in the group to add their favourite movies or shows to the list.​
  • Mewatch can offer recommendations based on the collective preferences of the group, suggesting new content that aligns with the group's interests.
  • Example: Frenzi app allows the users to share the watchlist with their friends and let other users join the share list. ​

User 
Engagement

Short Videos

  • Short videos allows users to view, create, and share brief video clips, typically ranging from a few seconds to a few minutes. ​
  • Mewatch can display short clips, trailers, behind-the-scenes footage, and highlights of any movies or series.​
  • YouTube Shorts generates more than 30 billion views per day. As of December 2022, videos under 1 minute made up to 55% of the YouTube views.​

User-generated content​

  • It means that  the end-user, they become active contributors instead of passive consumers. ​
  • RCTV,  Spanish content video catalogs launched the #RCTVChallenge, a contest where subscribers have a chance to win 50 coins for imitating their favourite character within the category they choose.​
  • This innovative feature resulted in +250,000 subscribers in first month and increased 80% engagement on the app.​​

Ratings and Reviews

  • Implement a robust system for users to rate and review content.​
  • Display aggregate ratings and popular reviews to help users make informed choices.​
  • According to a report on US movie-going habits by research firm Nielsen, 80% of film viewers will consult critics' reviews when they're considering what to watch.​
  • Example: Disney+ Hotstar has recently launched "review" feature which allows user to cast their rating for contents.​

User 
Experience

Brand connect​

  • Brand integration in live streaming enables direct user interaction with brands, fostering mutually beneficial relationships between viewers, advertisers, and streaming platforms.
  • Mewatch can integrate with Apple brand into its live-streaming app to display Apple products. 
  • Example: Mahindra, a leading Indian conglomerate, sponsors ICC CW 2023 on Disney+ Hotstar, which is why Hotstar has integrated the Mahindra brand into its live-streaming app. Viewers interested in buying a Mahindra vehicle can easily explore and book a test drive while watching the live match without switching to another website or app.​

Personalized 'remind- me' notifications​

  • Allows user to be notified on event of new releases. ​
  • Users can "Add to watchlist" in case of not being able to watch during the time of receiving the notification.​
  • Example: Disney+ Hotstar allows users to add notified new releases to watchlist for the user to watch later and not miss it. Fancode, an OTT app specifically catering to sports, allows user to select the notifications from their favorite team, matches etc.​
