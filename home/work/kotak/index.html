Project Overview
About Invesco Mutual Fund
India’s rapid digitization over the last decade has transformed how we order food, book tickets, and shop, but banking remained largely unchanged. While many banks made incremental improvements, Kotak Mahindra Bank took a bold leap, redefining digital banking in India.
By focusing on clutter-free design, reducing user complexity, and delivering a seamless digital experience, Kotak set out to revolutionize how millions of Indians interact with their finances.
Time frame
3 months for desktop and mobile responsive design.
Team
I led the design process alongside the Project Manager, Design Head, and UI Designers, stakeholders and development team to ensure seamless front-end and back-end integration.
My Role
As a Design Lead, I developed a scalable design system to ensure brand consistency across web and mobile platforms while optimizing information architecture to reduce complexity and enhance accessibility. By collaborating with TCS’s development team, I ensured a seamless design-to-development handoff and conducted usability testing to validate designs through real-world user interactions.
Challenges
  • 26 Different Services: Each service operated on a separate website, creating a disjointed user experience.
  • Multiple Websites: Users had to switch between different websites for each service, making it harder to navigate.
  • Limited Cross-Service Exploration: The lack of a unified platform reduced awareness and engagement with other services.
Unified Dashboard
Designed a single platform combining all 26 services, removing the need to switch between websites and ensuring a seamless, consistent experience.
Solution for Managing Multiple Accounts
  • Diverse Account Types: Addressed user needs for deposit, credit, and investment accounts in one place.
  • User-Centric Research: Conducted in-depth research to understand key user requirements.
  • Unified Account View: Developed a dashboard mechanism to easily review, monitor, and manage all accounts in one interface.

Grouping for easy to search
We had a feeling based on the laws of grouping from Gestalt psychology that understanding and exploiting the abilities of the human visual system is an important part of the design of usable user interfaces (Rosenholtz et al., 2009). Accordingly, we categorized required and correlated information and grouped them for ease of use.
Customizable Service Cards:
Enabled users to personalize their dashboard with service-specific cards, making frequently used services easier to access.
Personalized Recommendations via Integrated Messaging
  • Tailored Advice and Tips: Delivered personalized financial advice to help users improve their financial well-being.
  • Predictive Insights: Provided recommendations based on user behavior and financial patterns.
  • Empowering Financial Growth: Focused on enhancing users' financial status and catering to their individual needs.
Control Centre for User Settings
  • Increased Sense of Control: Designed a user-friendly control center, enhancing the feeling of control, similar to a reward mechanism (Leotti & Delgado, 2011).
  • Simplified Account Management: Enabled easy viewing and updating of account, card, and security settings in one place.
  • Aligned with Core Principles: This feature strengthened the system’s usability and user satisfaction by offering a seamless management experience.
To enable customers to pay routine bills effortlessly, our online banking design offers the ‘upcoming payment’ widget as a solution. Our goal was to spotlight key actions the user needs to take and preserve a minimal visual density to ensure ease of use.
Frictionless onboarding
It was important for users to also adopt the digital platform with ease, so we created an onboarding experience that helped familiarise the user with the new online banking journey while showing them personalised actions and laying out the standout features. This ensured users can use the new banking experience without any human error, resulting in a superior experience across the site.
Accessible anywhere, anytime
  • Time-Sensitive Transactions: Designed the system to support critical payments even in areas with poor data coverage.
  • Accessibility Anytime, Anywhere: Created a banking experience that ensures uninterrupted service regardless of location.
  • Building Trust: Prioritized reliability to strengthen user confidence in Kotak’s banking system.
