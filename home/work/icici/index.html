The Context
The Ozone app project was undertaken for internal use by the staff of ICICI bank. It was designed to facilitate optimum use of ICICI’s campus services. Out of these, it turned out, the cafeteria was the most used service by almost every employee! The design language was refined using three unique devices used most to run the application, the employees’ personal devices, the operators’ desktop computers and the common cafeteria TV.
