Project Overview
About Invesco Mutual Fund
Invesco Asset Management (India) provides mutual funds and portfolio solutions to individuals, corporates, and institutions. As part of the US-headquartered Invesco, a global leader in investment solutions, it delivers innovative financial services.
Time frame
4 month timeline for 3 apps experience.
Team
A cross-functional team comprising product managers, subject experts, UI designers, and developers collaborated to create a seamless digital experience for 40K+ distributors.
My Role
I led the design of the Invesco MF Distributor App, driving it from concept to delivery. I worked closely with stakeholders to define requirements, align on strategy, and secure approvals. Managing two Sr. UI designers, I ensured a cohesive design approach.
Challenges
In asset management, distributors and relationship managers act as trusted advisors, making investor relationships crucial. Previously reliant on multiple platforms for processes and documentation, they needed a centralized, all-in-one tool to manage operations, support investors, track income, and drive business growth.
Design Goal
"Our design goal was to let numbers take center stage, ensuring every key metric was clearly presented. By removing distractions and simplifying the layout, we created a focused experience where even the smallest change in numbers could be easily tracked and leveraged for impactful decisions."

From Complexity to Simplicity: Redefining Initiate Transactions
Priorities distributor needs by creating a fast, intuitive, and flexible transaction initiation process, driving business growth and enhancing user satisfaction for all stakeholders.
Distributors can initiate investment for investors. So investors just pay and don’t need to fill out the whole form and then invest. Investor skip all the steps and just directly invest in fund.
This intuitive placement streamlines workflows, saves navigation time, and helps distributors manage transactions effortlessly to focus on business growth.
Empowers distributors to save time and manage multiple transactions seamlessly, removing navigation hurdles.
Avoids long, frustrating scrolls by enabling fast navigation with a familiar mobile contact list experience.
Distributors handle transactions daily, so familiar options and pre-filled choices speed up investments, reduce manual input, and save valuable time.
Distributors can update specific details directly, avoiding unnecessary form navigation and saving time with a seamless, focused editing experience.
A step-by-step progress bar keeps distributors informed and in control, showing completed steps, current status, and remaining actions for a seamless investment process.

Unfolding AUM Data: Empowering Distributors to Analyze, Manage, and Act Effortlessly
Blocks
Overwhelming data presentation made it difficult for distributors to focus on relevant insights. With cluttered and unorganized information increasing decision fatigue and hindering goal-oriented actions.
The AUM section was strategically designed to provide distributors with a seamless, step-by-step flow of essential information, minimizing cognitive load while empowering them with actionable insights. Instead of overwhelming users with all data upfront, the design unfolds relevant details progressively, enabling a focused, task-driven experience.
This strategy combines clarity, hierarchy, and user empowerment, enabling distributors to analyze, manage, and act on AUM data effortlessly while maintaining focus on their specific goals.
Blocks
The section occupied excessive space, limiting room for other critical information.
Optimised the layout to focus on key metrics and stay informed without navigating complex data.
Blocks
Difficulty tracking recent transactions efficiently and lack of upfront actions complicating task resolution.
Key numbers and actions upfront enable track and act real-time and streamlined workflows, boosting efficiency and ensuring no missed opportunities.
Blocks
Difficulty navigating frequently used services repeatedly
Quick access to frequently used tools and streamlines workflows, enhances usability.
Here’s how these sections contribute to an efficient, fully-loaded dashboard
Blocking Exposure, Unlocking Control
Blocks
Privacy Concerns: Shared access in offices risks exposing brokerage details.
Sensitive Information Exposure: Income data may be unintentionally visible to investors.
  • Introduced a PIN-protected window, Maintains privacy, builds trust
  • This solution demonstrates a user-centered approach, addressing privacy concerns with a simple yet effective design to enhance trust and usability for distributors.
Streamlined communication reflects Invesco’s commitment to support
Blocks
Scattered Communication Channels: Distributors often had to rely on multiple platforms (email, messaging apps, or calls) to connect with their Regional Manager, leading to inefficiencies and time loss.
  • Centralized Access: Added a dedicated "Your RM" section in the top header for immediate visibility and quick access. ​
  • Integrated email, messaging, and video call into one interface for seamless, user-centric communication, eliminating the need for external tools
Mini-Dashboards for Quick Scheme Insights
  • Strategic Use of Hierarchy and Design Principles  Designed the scheme cards with a clear hierarchy, highlighting essential performance metrics like returns, NAV, and fund performance at the top to ensure distributors quickly grasp key details.
  • Enhanced Search and Filter Functionality Quick Results, Integrated easy-to-tab filters with dynamic results, enabling distributors to narrow down options quickly and alter selections effortlessly.
